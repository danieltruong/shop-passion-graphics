Orders & payment

How do I pay?
Checkout runs on Stripe. You can pay with major credit cards, Visa or Mastercard debit, and Apple Pay or Google Pay on supported devices. We never see or store your card details.
Can I change or cancel my order?
Email us as soon as you can. If your order hasn't shipped yet, we'll change it or cancel it with a full refund.
Which currency are prices in?
All prices are in Canadian dollars (CAD). If your card is in another currency, your bank handles the conversion and may charge a small fee.

Shipping

How long does shipping take?
Orders are packed by hand and ship within 3–5 business days. Delivery times by region are on the shipping page.
Do you ship internationally?
Right now we ship within Canada and to the US. If you're somewhere else, email us and we'll see what we can do.
How do I track my order?
You'll get a tracking link by email when your order ships.

Returns & exchanges

Can I return something?
Yes. Unused items in their original condition can be returned within 14 days of delivery. Custom and personalized pieces are final sale. Details on the returns page.
My order arrived damaged. What now?
Sorry about that! Email a photo to [your email] within 7 days of delivery and we'll send a replacement or refund you. No need to send it back.

Why does mine look a little different from the photo?
Everything is made by hand, so small differences in colour and shape are normal. No two are exactly alike.

Sometimes! Email [your email] with what you have in mind. Custom pieces are final sale.
